# LRU and intervention checkpoint index

Final policy checkpoints used in [the 16-bin recall results](recall_density_16_main_final/REPORT.md). Each link points to an Equinox `model.eqx` file; its adjacent `config.json` records the training configuration. All listed policies were trained for 98,304 steps. See the [measurement manifest](recall_density_16_main_final/manifest.json) for SHA-256 hashes, trajectory results, and provenance.

The four conditions are plain LRU, default reverse-KL information bottleneck (KL), next-observation and reward prediction (Prediction), and five-level post-activation finite scalar quantization (FSQ). MDP and POMDP are separately trained policies.
